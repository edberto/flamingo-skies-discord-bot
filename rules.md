# 🌅 Welcome to Flamingo Skies Pier
A small indie studio making Roblox games. Keep it friendly so everyone enjoys the sky. 🦩

**1. Be kind.** No harassment, hate speech, slurs or personal attacks. Disagree with ideas, not people.
**2. Keep it safe for everyone.** No NSFW, gore or shock content, including in names, avatars and bios.
**3. Use the right channel.** Ideas go in your game's 💡・suggestions, bugs in 🐛・bug-report, everything else in chat.
**4. No spam or self-promotion.** No ad links, server invites or DM advertising without asking a moderator first.
**5. No scams, cheats or exploits.** No account or Robux trading, no sharing exploits. Found one in our games? Report it privately to a Moderator; don't post it publicly.
**6. No pinging staff for nothing.** Use @everyone or role pings only for real emergencies.
**7. Follow Discord's and Roblox's rules.** [Discord Terms](https://discord.com/terms) · [Community Guidelines](https://discord.com/guidelines) · [Roblox Terms](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/articles/115004647846)
**8. Moderators have the final say.** If you disagree with a decision, DM a Moderator calmly rather than arguing in public.

Breaking the rules can mean a warning, a timeout, or a ban, depending on how serious it is.
